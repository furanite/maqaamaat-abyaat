# Hearing the Maqamat · المقامات

A 28-day course for beginners who want to hear and sing the maqamat of Quran recitation. You don't need to read music.

**Open it:** https://furanite.github.io/maqaamaat-abyaat/

- Days 1 to 5 train the ear: high and low, the home sound, and tiny, middle and full steps.
- Then Bayati, Hijaz, Rast, Saba, Nahawand and Kurd, each with a staircase you can tap, a sung "aah" example, an "Echo me" call-and-response drill and singing exercises.
- One test a day. It runs from easy to hard, explains every answer and opens the next day once you pass.
- Free practice and reference cards for all six maqamat.
- Three colour themes: Milk & Gold, Emerald and Night.

It is a single `index.html` with no build step. All sound is made in the browser with the Web Audio API. Progress is saved in your browser's local storage.
